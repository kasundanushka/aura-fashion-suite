Admin Catalog & Data Ingestion

Administrators provision garment SKUs (categories, pricing, stock) and upload sales CSVs. The system cleans and indexes data with audit logging.

Primary Purpose · Administrator Portal

Total Governance, SKU Catalog
& Pipeline Retraining.

The Admin Control Center (admin.html) is the mission-critical core of AURA. Built exclusively for system administrators, it orchestrates the machine learning pipeline, provisions products, and safeguards operational integrity.

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Provision, promote, demote, or delete accounts with protected root administrator privileges.
  • SKU & Catalog Provisioning: Add new garment SKUs, assign wholesale costs, retail pricing, and initial warehouse stock levels.
  • One-Click Pipeline Retrain: Trigger real-time data cleaning, feature engineering, and model refresh with instant alert counters.
  • Security Audit Logging: Automatic IP-tracked audit trails logging every administrative and authentication action.
  • Dataset Export: Instant CSV downloads of clean sales history, feature matrices, and reorder cost matrices.

Customer-Facing Innovation · Buyer Portal

3D Avatar Sizing
& AI Garment Virtual Try-On.

The Buyer Section (buyer.html) transforms online shopping into an interactive 3D fitting session. Shoppers customize exact anatomical body models, upload garments, and visualize how clothes drape before purchasing — slashing returns and giving retailers real sizing demand telemetry.

  • Tailored 3D Body Models: Interactive Male and Female base avatars with dynamic physique adjustment.
  • 7-Point Precision Measurements: Height, chest, waist, hips, shoulder width, arm length, and leg length (in cm).
  • AI Garment Segmentation: Automatic background removal, category detection (Shirt, Dress, Pants), and color palette extraction.
  • Front & Back View Simulation: Automatically detects or synthesizes front and back garment perspectives for 360° try-on.
